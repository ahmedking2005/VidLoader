Privacy Policy — VidLoader

Last updated: 2026-11-22

VidLoader (package name: com.ahmed.vidloader, Arabic name: منزّل الفيديو) is a free video downloader app for Android developed by Ahmed ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. It applies to version 1.12.0 and later unless an updated policy is published.

The short version

  • No account. VidLoader never asks you to register or sign in.
  • No personal data collected by the developer. We do not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information. We operate no server that receives your data.
  • Everything you do in the app stays on your device.
  • One third-party SDK: Unity Ads, used only to show the app's light advertising.

Information stored on your device

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted to the developer:

  • Files you download, and the download queue (including automatic-retry state)
  • Your link history, library tags, and app settings
  • Channels and playlists you choose to monitor, and scheduled downloads
  • An optional cookies.txt file only if you import one yourself from the app's settings. It stays on your device and is used solely to access the links you ask the app to download.

You can erase this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Links you paste

When you paste or share a link, the app connects directly from your device to the website hosting that content in order to fetch the media you requested. That website receives the same information any web request carries (such as your IP address and the link requested) and is governed by its own privacy policy. The developer does not see, log, or receive the links you use.

Advertising — Unity Ads

VidLoader is free and is supported by light advertising provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity Ads is the only third-party advertising or analytics SDK in the app. To serve and measure ads, Unity may process data such as your device's advertising ID, device model, operating system version, IP address, and ad interaction data, in accordance with Unity's own privacy policy:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Ad policy in the app: a small banner on the home screen, and an occasional interstitial shown only after a download completes (never before, and at most once every 10 minutes). The app contains no other analytics, tracking, or data-collection SDKs.

You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to work: network access (to fetch the media you request and to load ads), storage/media access (to save your downloads where you choose), notifications (to show download progress, only if you allow them), and foreground-service permission (so downloads continue while you use other apps).

Children

VidLoader is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address as this document, with a new "Last updated" date.
